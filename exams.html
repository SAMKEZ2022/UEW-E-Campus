Welcome

This space lets you enter or import student scores, course by course, for any department and any level of the university.

Go to the tab Score Entry, then choose a department, a level and a course in turn: the list of students concerned (with their index numbers) is displayed automatically, ready to be graded by hand or through an Excel file.

Score Entry by Course

Printing Transcripts

Choose a department, a level, a semester and then a student: the transcript is issued semester by semester and includes only the courses of that semester (graded or not) together with the average and credit hours of that semester. It can then be printed directly.

Student academic record

Rebuilds a student's complete academic history, semester by semester and course by course (grouped by module, with credit hours), including scores obtained in a previous programme if they re-registered with an old index number. Also includes their cumulative attendance time in live lectures and the number of assignments submitted.
