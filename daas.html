Pending registrations

This space lets you manage the online registration applications submitted by applicants on the public portal registration.html : approving or rejecting applications, setting the registration period, and exporting the list of applications.

When an application is approved, the student account is created automatically (login email, temporary password and index number generated), and the applicant finds the login details when tracking their application.

Online registrations

Applications submitted by applicants from the public portal registration.html. On approval, the student account is created automatically (login email, temporary password and index number generated), and the applicant finds the login details when tracking their application.

Registration period

Outside this period, the public form shows a refusal message and accepts no new application. Leave a date empty to set no limit on that side (e.g. leave the end date empty for registration open until further notice).
